# Research Gap & Academic Foundation

## 1. Research Background & Base Paper

- **Base Paper Title**: *"Digital Learning Platform for Rural School Students"*
- **Authors**: Chapke, N., Naladkar, M., Belgude, N., & Satav, A.
- **Journal**: International Journal of Engineering Research & Technology (IJERT), 2026.

Existing research in rural digital learning emphasizes distributing online study materials, conducting basic multiple-choice quizzes, and tracking overall lesson completion.

---

## 2. Identified Research Gap

While existing LMS solutions deliver educational content and progress metrics, they present key limitations when deployed in rural contexts:

1. **Lack of Granular Weak Topic Detection**: Conventional platforms record overall quiz marks (e.g. 7/10) without isolating specific conceptual sub-topics (e.g., Fractions Addition vs Fraction Multiplication).
2. **Missing Automated Remedial Loop**: Students are notified of failure without an automated engine prescribing targeted study resources based on individual diagnostic scores.
3. **No Telangana Curriculum Alignment**: Existing solutions do not cater to Telangana State Board School Education (Classes 6–10 in Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, English, and Telugu).
4. **Lack of Telugu-Medium Support**: Rural Telangana learners struggle with monolingual English interfaces.
5. **Absence of Empirical Pre/Post Test Research Evaluation**: Existing platforms lack built-in modules comparing baseline performance before and after personalized intervention to measure learning gain percentage.

---

## 3. Main Research Contribution of This Project

This major project resolves the identified research gaps through an integrated **Weak Topic Detection and Personalized Learning Engine** for Telangana rural school students.

```
Student Attempt
      ↓
Grading & Topic Mapping
      ↓
Threshold Evaluation (<40% WEAK | 40-60% NEEDS IMPROVEMENT | >60% GOOD)
      ↓
Automated Recommendation Generation (Notes, Low-Bandwidth Video, Practice Sets)
      ↓
Targeted Remediation & Offline Study
      ↓
Post-Test Retest Execution
      ↓
Pre/Post Delta Score Gain Calculation (+42%)
      ↓
Research Evaluation Dashboard Output
```

---

## 4. Key Academic Innovations

- **Transparent Rule-Based Recommendation Algorithm**: Transparent evaluation thresholds (`<40%`, `40–60%`, `>60%`) avoiding opaque black-box machine learning models.
- **Bilingual Interface Engine**: English and Telugu dictionary context switching across student, quiz, and recommendation modules.
- **Low-Bandwidth Rural Optimization**: 2G/3G low-data stream badges and single-click offline text/notes download capability.
